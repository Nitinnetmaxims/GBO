Introduction

Understanding Social Media Management

Social Media Management is the process of creating, publishing, promoting, and managing content across social media channels like Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Twitter.

It goes beyond just uploading photos; it requires a strategic approach to build brand equity, engage followers, and turn them into loyal customers.
