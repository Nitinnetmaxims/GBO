What Are Technical SEO Services?

Technical SEO refers to the optimizations performed on your website and server to help search engine spiders crawl and index your site more effectively, directly impacting your organic rankings.

The Foundation

  • Fix Broken Architecture
  • Eliminate Crawl Waste
  • Maximize Page Speed

Workflow

Our Technical SEO Process

A rigorous, step-by-step approach to identifying, prioritizing, and resolving technical bottlenecks.

01

Comprehensive Audit

Running enterprise-level crawls to identify every single error code, redirect loop, and indexation issue.

02

Site Speed Analysis

Evaluating TTFB, FCP, LCP, and CLS scores to find optimization opportunities for faster loading.

03

Architecture Review

Mapping out your internal linking structure to ensure link equity flows to priority pages.

04

Prioritization & Strategy

Categorizing issues by severity and organic impact to create an actionable development roadmap.

05

Crawlability Fixes

Updating robots.txt, XML sitemaps, and fixing broken links so Googlebot can crawl efficiently.

06

Indexation Control

Implementing canonical tags and noindex directives to cure duplicate content and thin pages.

07

Code Optimization

Minifying CSS/JS, optimizing images, and reducing server response times for blazing fast speed.

08

Schema Implementation

Adding JSON-LD structured data to win rich snippets, FAQ dropdowns, and review stars in SERPs.

09

Monitoring & Maintenance

Continuous crawl monitoring via Google Search Console to catch and fix new errors immediately.

On-page SEO focuses on optimizing the content and keywords that users see. Technical SEO focuses on the backend structure, code, and server settings that search engines see to crawl and index your site properly.

Many technical fixes require code-level or server-level changes. We can either work directly with your existing development team by providing clear instructions, or our in-house developers can implement the fixes for you.

A comprehensive enterprise-level audit usually takes between 2 to 4 weeks, depending on the size and complexity of your website architecture and CMS.

Core Web Vitals are specific user experience metrics determined by Google to measure a page's visual load speed (LCP), visual stability (CLS), and interactivity (INP). Failing these metrics can hold back your rankings.
