Frequently Asked Questions About Social Media Advertising

1 What is Social Media Advertising?
Social media advertising involves paying social networks like Facebook, LinkedIn, or Instagram to display your promoted content to a highly targeted audience based on demographics and interests.
2 How long does it take to see results?
Unlike SEO, paid social media advertising can generate traffic and leads almost immediately after launching the campaign, though the most optimal ROI usually occurs after 2-4 weeks of algorithmic learning.
3 Do you handle ad creatives and copywriting?
Yes! Our in-house team of designers and copywriters will create all the visual assets, videos, and compelling text required for your campaigns.
4 Which platform is best for my business?
It depends heavily on your industry. B2B companies often thrive on LinkedIn, while D2C ecommerce brands see massive returns on Instagram and Facebook. We will determine the optimal platform mix during strategy planning.
5 How do you track conversions?
We integrate advanced tracking pixels and server-side APIs on your website to ensure every lead, sale, or sign-up generated from an ad is perfectly attributed.
