Introduction

What Social Media Advertising Means

Social Media Advertising is the process of paying social networks (like Facebook, LinkedIn, or TikTok) to deliver your content directly to highly specific user groups.

Unlike organic posting, paid social guarantees reach and allows you to target users based on their demographics, interests, job titles, and past behaviors.

Why Paid Social Works

  • Instant Visibility
  • Hyper-Targeting
  • Advanced Retargeting
  • Measurable ROI
